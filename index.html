The Mission

History keeps a shortlist. We're building the long one.

Most credit goes to whoever was easiest to put a face to — a founder, a front-man, a famous name attached late. Behind the Breakthrough exists to find the people who were actually in the room, verify their part in the record, and give their story a permanent home.

  1. 01
    ResearchWe dig through patents, lab notes, interviews, and primary sources to verify who actually did the work.
  2. 02
    Build the recordVerified cases go into a public, citable database — built to last, not to trend.
  3. 03
    Tell the storyShort-form video and long-form writing turn each case into something people actually watch and share.
